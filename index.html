01 / Featured project / 008

Memory Drift.

NEW · 2026
《忘了自己是什么》 · 互动装置 + 像素游戏 + TD 影像What Was I Again? · Installation + pixel game + TD film

一件围绕“遗忘是缺陷，还是自我保护”的互动装置。观众先看一张照片，再在旅途中收集它的碎片，并被反复问到同一个问题。 An interactive installation asking whether forgetting is a defect or a form of self-protection. Visitors study one photograph, collect its fragments on a journey, and are asked the same question again and again.

“照片里，到底有几个人？”“How many people were in the photo?”

480 × 270 像素画布 · 横屏 16:9 · 一根摇杆、六个按钮的展陈机台480 × 270 pixel canvas · 16:9 landscape · one joystick, six buttons on the exhibition cabinet

照片被切成五条固定位置的碎片：撞到障碍会丢失最新的一片，干扰会让最旧的一片变模糊。The photo is split into five fixed strips: a collision loses the newest piece, interference blurs the oldest one.

  1. 看一张照片，先回答一次Look once, answer once

    3 · 4 · 5 · 不确定。第一次回答会在任何暗示之前被记录下来。3 · 4 · 5 · Not sure. The first answer is recorded before any suggestion appears.

  2. 在路上收集、遗失、保护碎片Collect, lose and protect fragments

    向右走、跳跃，按住保护挡住一次冲击。保留下来的碎片，就是结尾你能看到的部分。Walk right, jump, hold Protect to block one hit. Whatever you keep is what you get to see at the end.

  3. 两个人说：是五个Two people say: five

    虚构角色 Lin 与 Rowan 会引用你的第二次回答并彼此回应；记录墙上则是此前观众真实的回答。Fictional characters Lin and Rowan quote your second answer and reply to each other; the record wall shows real answers from earlier visitors.

  4. 揭晓原图，不打分Reveal the original — no score

    先回看你留下的碎片，再揭晓原图，并置三次回答；最后谈曼德拉效应，把“遗忘是否有价值”留给观众。Review the pieces you kept, then reveal the original beside your three answers. A closing card on the Mandela effect leaves the value of forgetting open.
