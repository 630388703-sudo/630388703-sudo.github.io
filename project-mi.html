Project Overview

MAZE / 迷 is a 2-minute 30-second AI-generated film created for the Perfect World Training Camp Game Jam, with the theme “迷” (maze / lost / obsession). Rather than building a playable game, I chose to express the game concept through AI film — using the medium itself as a reflection of the theme.

The film explores the experience of being lost — not spatially, but cognitively and emotionally. The AI-generated visuals blur the line between real and constructed, between memory and imagination, creating a space where the viewer experiences the disorientation of “迷” directly.

Concept & Direction

🌀
Core Concept
The AI-generated image is itself a maze: coherent enough to follow, strange enough to disorient. By choosing AI video as the medium for a game about 迷 (maze/lost), the form becomes the content.
Narrative Structure
Three-Phase Arc

Entry: familiar spaces that gradually distort. Immersion: the deep maze — where orientation is impossible. Resolution: emergence — but into a different space than where you entered. The ending is not an exit; it's a new beginning of not-knowing.

Visual Language
Coherent Incoherence

Each AI-generated shot is visually beautiful but spatially impossible. Corridors that don't connect. Rooms that repeat with small variations. The editing rhythm shifts from slow and contemplative to rapid and disorienting as the film progresses.

03

AIGC Workflow

The film was produced using a four-tool AI video pipeline — each tool selected for specific visual characteristics that served the maze theme.

Kling
Architecture & Space

Used for architectural environments — corridors, staircases, vast interior spaces. Kling's tendency toward detailed, physically plausible structures made it ideal for the 'almost-real' quality needed in the entry phase.

Runway
Motion & Transition

Used for motion-heavy sequences and transitions between spaces. Runway's fluid motion generation created the sensation of moving through the maze without clear endpoints.

Pika + Luma
Detail & Texture

Pika handled close-up detail shots and texture transitions. Luma contributed its distinctive quality of light and material in the emergence sequence at the end of the film.

Outcome & Reflection

MAZE / 迷 was the project that taught me to think of AI tools as a director thinks about cinematography — not as generators that produce content, but as cinematographic choices that shape what the viewer feels. The tool selection is the visual language selection.
