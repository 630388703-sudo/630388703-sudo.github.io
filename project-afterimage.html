Project Overview

AFTERIMAGE / 残像 is a Unity 2D cyberpunk narrative puzzle game built for the Lilith Game Jam's "My AI Teammate" track. The player takes on the role of ECHO — a detective in a dual-layer city — whose AI companion "Shadow" mirrors the player's own behavioural data in real time.

The core twist: Shadow is not a separate entity. It is a reflection of you. Every choice the player makes shapes Shadow's behaviour — and Shadow's behaviour reshapes the player's understanding of themselves.

"你一直在和自己对话。"  You have always been talking to yourself.

Design Problem

As AI companions become more present in games — from voiced NPCs to adaptive behaviour systems — a critical UX question emerges:

❓
Design Question
How might an AI teammate influence player trust and decision-making — and what happens when that AI is a mirror of the player themselves?
Player Challenge
Trust vs. Suspicion

Players must decide how much to trust Shadow's guidance — not knowing that Shadow's behaviour directly reflects their own past choices. This creates a loop of self-doubt and revelation.

Design Challenge
Invisible Feedback

The feedback loop between player action and AI response needed to be felt — not explained. The UI and narrative had to carry this without breaking immersion or over-signalling.

03

Research & References

I drew on both game design references and psychological theory to ground the AI companion system.

Game Reference
Disco Elysium

Internalized voices as dialogue partners. Fragmented identity as a gameplay mechanic. The player's psyche as the battleground.

Game Reference
Hellblade

Psychosis as narrative form. Voices that blur the line between guidance and threat. Audio design as psychological UX.

Theory
Mirror Stage

Lacan's theory of the mirror stage — identity formed through recognition of a reflected image — directly inspired Shadow's core mechanic.

💡
Key Insight
The most effective AI companions in games are not the ones with the most capabilities — they are the ones that make players feel something about themselves.
04

Interaction Design

The game is built around three interlocking interaction systems, each designed to deepen the player's emotional and cognitive investment in Shadow.

01
Dual-Layer World — Tab Switch System

The city has two layers — the polished surface and the hidden underside. Players switch between them using a Tab key system, each layer revealing different information and different facets of Shadow's personality. The same space reads entirely differently depending on which layer you inhabit.

Spatial UX Information Architecture C# / Unity
02
NPC Dialogue System — Branching + Behaviour Tracking

Three fully scripted NPCs with JSON-driven dialogue trees — each with conditional triggers based on the player's prior choices. Every dialogue selection is logged to a behaviour profile that Shadow reads from. The system was designed so that players rarely notice they are being tracked.

Dialogue Design Behaviour Tracking JSON / C#
03
AI Mirror — Shadow's Adaptive Response

Shadow's personality, tone, and even visual glitch effects shift based on the player's accumulated behavioural data. A player who makes cold, analytical choices sees a more detached Shadow. A player who shows empathy sees Shadow become warmer. The player is, in effect, writing Shadow's character with every action.

07

Technical Implementation

Building a multi-scene Unity 2D project independently required careful architecture decisions — especially around state management, scene transitions, and the custom tooling that enabled rapid iteration.

Architecture
Singleton + DontDestroyOnLoad

Core managers (GameState, DialogueManager, BehaviourLog) persist across scenes via DontDestroyOnLoad. Singleton pattern ensures a single source of truth for player data throughout the game session.

Custom Tooling
AfterimageSetupTool.cs

A custom Unity Editor tool built to automate repetitive scene setup tasks — wiring managers, assigning prefabs, configuring CanvasGroup states. Reduced per-scene setup time by ~60%.

Solved Challenges
Key Engineering Problems

Resolved: recursive LoadScene conflicts, coroutine race conditions in dialogue transitions, CanvasGroup visibility management across async operations, and duplicate Singleton instantiation on scene reload.

Distribution
PC + WebGL Build

Optimised for both PC standalone and WebGL (itch.io). WebGL build required texture compression adjustments and memory management rewrites to stay within browser constraints.

08

Outcome & Reflection

AFTERIMAGE was submitted to the Lilith Game Jam and distributed as both a PC build and WebGL version. The project represents my most technically complex independent work to date.

Beyond technical execution, the project taught me how to design felt experience — emotion that isn't stated but implied through system design, environmental storytelling, and the gap between what the player expects and what they discover.

  • Designed and implemented 10+ fully playable scenes as solo developer
  • Built custom Editor tooling that reduced iteration time significantly
  • Created an AI companion system that responds to invisible player data
  • Shipped dual distribution targets: PC standalone + WebGL
  • Integrated AIGC assets (Gemini, Kling, Runway) into production pipeline
🔮
What I'd Do Differently
Earlier playtesting — ideally from Scene 3 onwards, not just at the end. The mirror mechanic needed external eyes earlier to validate that the reveal felt earned rather than arbitrary. I'd also invest more time in audio design: the emotional weight of the twist lived or died on sound.
