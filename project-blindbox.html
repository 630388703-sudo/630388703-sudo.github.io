Project Overview

An independently designed advertising campaign for MINISO's blind box series. The brief was to communicate the core emotional appeal of blind boxes — the thrill of surprise, the joy of collecting, the tactile pleasure of the unknown — through both print and video formats.

Design Direction

❓
Design Question
How might visual design communicate the surprise and collectibility of blind boxes — the moment before you know, not the moment after?
Visual Direction
The Moment of Not-Yet-Knowing

Rather than showing the blind box contents (which spoils the surprise), the campaign focuses on the closed box — the weight, the possibility, the anticipation. The visual language references gift-wrapping aesthetics and collector's item presentation.

Brand Alignment
MINISO's Playful Precision

MINISO sits at the intersection of accessible and desirable — products that feel premium without being expensive. The campaign needed to honour this: playful enough for impulse purchase, considered enough to feel like a genuine collectible.

03

Poster Series

Three A3 poster designs at 300dpi, RGB. Each poster explores a different facet of the blind box experience — curiosity, collection, and connection.

Blind Box Advertising — Poster Series · A3 · 300dpi · RGB

04

Video Advertisement

A short video advertisement bringing the blind box experience to motion — capturing the tactile details of unboxing, the suspense of the reveal, and the emotional response of discovery. Edited in Premiere Pro with motion graphics elements from After Effects.

Blind Box Advertising — Video Ad · Premiere Pro + After Effects

05

Outcome & Reflection

This project developed my ability to work within brand constraints while still making design decisions that feel genuinely considered rather than templated. The constraint of 'not showing the product' forced more interesting visual thinking than a straightforward product showcase would have.

  • Designed 3 A3 posters at 300dpi, RGB, print-ready
  • Produced one video advertisement in Premiere Pro + After Effects
  • Built complete visual system consistent across print and video
  • Applied MINISO brand language while adding original visual direction
