Visual Novel · Game Design

Growth Pain

A visual novel exploring emotional growth, social pressure and self-discovery through interactive storytelling.

Visual Novel Game Design Narrative Design UI Design

Project Overview

Growth Pain is a choice-driven visual novel that follows a young student navigating academic stress, family expectations, and the quiet moments of self-discovery in between. Players make decisions that shape the protagonist's emotional journey — choosing whether to speak up, retreat, or find a third path.

The project was born from real experiences and genuine empathy for what it feels like to grow up under pressure. Every dialogue branch was crafted to feel authentic, not prescriptive.

My Role

  • Narrative design — story structure, branching dialogue, emotional arc mapping
  • UI/UX design — interface layout, typography system, interaction flows
  • Visual direction — color palettes, scene compositions, character expression design
  • Storyboard — key scene illustration and sequence planning

Story Design

The story unfolds across three chapters, each representing a stage of internal conflict: Denial, Reckoning, and Acceptance. The branching structure allows players to revisit earlier choices and see how small decisions compound over time.

Tone was carefully calibrated — warm yet melancholic, illustrated with a soft color palette that shifts between hopeful peach tones and cooler blue-greys to reflect the protagonist's emotional state.

UI Design

The interface was designed to stay out of the story's way. Clean rounded panels, gentle fade transitions, and a minimal HUD keep immersion intact. The dialogue box uses soft shadows and warm cream tones consistent with the illustration style.
